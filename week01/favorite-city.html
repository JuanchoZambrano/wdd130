My favorite city! - Medellin

Medellín is Colombia’s second-largest city, nestled in a lush mountain valley. Known as the “City of Eternal Spring,” it has a mild climate, vibrant culture, innovative public transportation, and a lively arts and nightlife scene. Its weather is one of the best reasons why I would like to live in there. It’s especially famous for its transformation from a troubled past into a modern, dynamic city.
